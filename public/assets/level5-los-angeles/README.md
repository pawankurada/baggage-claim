# Stage 5: Los Angeles

`src/los-angeles.js` draws only from x=6600 to the end of the world, including the existing final landing. Previous stages, collisions, pickups and ending behavior are unchanged.

`coast/santa-monica.svg` is an original vector pier/Ferris-wheel silhouette. Replace it through `losAngelesAssets.pier`.

Palms, pastel boulevard, tall HOLLYWOOD hillside lettering, a small Pride and Prejudice premiere poster, beach, animated water, supported lights and three quiet hearts are local Canvas scenery. One sunset sky palette spans the entire stage. The fifth bag's existing position overlooks the transition into the coast. The original finish flag, celebration, Open Your Letter button and full letter screen stay intact.
